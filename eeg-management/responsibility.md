# 使用者責任<br>User Responsibilities

## 使用規定 / Usage Regulations

| 中文 | English |
|------|---------|
| R605 腦波儀僅供所上教師研究，或於相關課程中使用。 | The R605 EEG is for faculty research or related courses only. |
| 請**準時**於預約時段內使用完畢，以免影響下一位。 | Finish within your reserved time so as not to affect the next user. |
| **嚴禁更改電腦軟體設定**；如須變更週邊，應先經負責教師認可，並於**當日復原**。 | **Changing computer/software settings is strictly prohibited**; peripheral changes need faculty approval and must be restored the **same day**. |
| 故障時立即通知管理人員或負責教師，**切勿自行拆裝**，並依[損害回報流程](damage.md)記錄。 | On failure, notify the manager/faculty immediately; **do not self-repair**, and record per the [report procedure](damage.md). |
| 結束後關妥**電腦、放大器及水電**，清理設備、電極帽與水槽，帶走個人物品、廢棄物與洗頭毛巾。 | Afterwards, turn off the **computer, amplifier, and water supply**, clean the equipment/cap/basin, and remove personal items, waste, and towels. |
| 請**自備隨身碟**存取資料，勿將檔案存於電腦；管理人員不負責保存數據。 | **Bring your own USB drive**; do not store files on the computer. The manager does not preserve data. |
| 每次使用前後**均須填寫「主試者自填確認項目表」**，記錄進出時間與儀器狀況。 | Fill out the user confirmation form **before and after each use**, recording times and equipment condition. |
| **自備實驗耗材**（導電膠、手套、擦手紙、酒精、棉棒、毛巾、洗髮精、注射筒等）。 | **Supply your own consumables** (conductive gel, gloves, paper towels, alcohol, cotton swabs, towels, shampoo, syringes, etc.). |

## 罰則 / Penalties

| 中文 | English |
|------|---------|
| 違規造成財產損失或公安意外者，視情節停權並負賠償責任。 | Violations causing property loss or safety incidents lead to suspension (by severity) and liability. |
| 未經允許自行使用儀器 → **停權一年**。 | Unauthorized use → **one-year suspension**. |
| 登記時間未到或未依規定取消預約 → 記違規一次。 | No-show or improper cancellation → one violation. |
| 合格者代他人預約 → 記違規一次。 | A certified user reserving for others → one violation. |
| 未填寫使用紀錄簿 → 記違規一次。 | Failing to fill out the usage log → one violation. |
| 將廢棄物留置實驗室 → 記違規一次。 | Leaving waste in the lab → one violation. |
| 第一次口頭警告；累計二次停權一個月；累計三次停權一年並通知 PI。 | First = verbal warning; two = one-month suspension; three = one-year suspension and PI notified. |
