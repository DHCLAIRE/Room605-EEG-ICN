# 儀器損害回報流程<br>Equipment Damage / Status Report

## EEG 使用狀況回報表 / EEG Status Report Form

每次將 EEG 設備歸還至 R611-1 後，**務必填寫回報表單**，並於**實驗後 2 日內**完成。
After returning the EEG equipment to R611-1, **always fill out the report form**, **within 2 days** post-experiment.

- 📋 [EEG 使用狀況回報表 / EEG Status Report Form](https://forms.gle/n683xq5ALLhKk6ms5)

| 中文 | English |
|------|---------|
| **實驗前**：須上傳 EEG 儀器**完整組裝後**的照片作為狀態佐證（**必填**）。 | **Before**: upload a photo of the **fully assembled** EEG setup as proof of condition (**mandatory**). |
| **實驗後**：照片非強制；但**若設備故障則須一併上傳**。 | **After**: a photo is optional — **unless the equipment malfunctioned, then it is mandatory**. |

## 設備故障處理 / Handling a Malfunction

| # | 中文 | English |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | **立即停止使用**，避免情況惡化。 | **Stop using immediately** to avoid worsening the issue. |
| 2 | **切勿自行拆裝或維修。** | **Do not** attempt to repair or disassemble it yourself. |
| 3 | 立即通知**管理人員**或**負責管理之教師**。 | Notify the **manager** or **responsible faculty** immediately. |
| 4 | 將故障情形記錄於「主試者自填確認項目表」，並於回報表單說明、上傳故障照片。 | Record the issue in the user's confirmation form, and describe it with a photo in the report form. |

> 誠實且即時的回報有助於快速修復與責任釐清。
> Honest, prompt reporting helps with quick repair and clear accountability.
